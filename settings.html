Privacy & Medical Disclaimer

Medical Disclaimer: BodyPro is a biometric telemetry and athletic performance logging tool designed for informational and educational purposes only. Calculations (including Adaptive TDEE, Mifflin-St Jeor BMR, 1RM Epley projections, CNS Readiness Scores, and Body Fat % estimations) do not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified physician or healthcare professional before starting any intensive exercise, nutrition, or supplementation protocol.

Data Privacy & Ownership (GDPR / CCPA / App Store 5.1.1): Your biometric logs, workouts, nutrition diary, and progress photos are stored in your local encrypted IndexedDB vault and synchronized privately to your authenticated Firebase Cloud document. BodyPro never sells or shares personal health data with third-party advertisers. Camera permissions are used strictly on-device for optical barcode/nutrition label scanning and optional progress photos.

Right to Erasure: You can export your complete JSON telemetry archive or permanently wipe your cloud & local account data at any time using the Data Management section below.
